About

Technology is a team sport.

BR

I am Baikuntha Rimal, Co-founder and Director of Addressgraph Nepal, where I also lead the team and look after technology.

I began as a Java and Android developer and, over more than ten years and several organisations, grew into leading projects, products and people. That path means I still care about how things are built, and I also care about why, and for whom.

Along the way I have been part of more than a hundred product developments, innovations and new features. Each one was delivered with a team whose skill and commitment I rely on, and whose work deserves most of the credit.

Our product Kataho has been recognised by the World Summit Awards (WSA), the APICTA Awards and the ICT Social Innovation Awards. I take those as encouragement to keep building carefully, and to keep the people who use our work at the centre of every decision.

I believe in clear communication, patient engineering and teams that feel trusted. Outside of work I enjoy mentoring younger engineers and learning from people who see problems differently from me.

How I work

A few principles

  1. Start with the user

    Good technology removes friction for someone. We keep asking who that someone is and whether we have really helped them.

  2. Build for the long run

    Prefer simple, maintainable systems over clever ones. Products have to last past the first release.

  3. Share the credit

    Results come from teams. I try to create the conditions where good people can do their best work, and to say so plainly.

  4. Keep learning

    The field moves quickly. Staying curious, and open to being wrong, has been the most useful habit of my career.
