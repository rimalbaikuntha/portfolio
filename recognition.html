Recognition

Honoured, and grateful.

Kataho has been recognised at national and international levels. These are team achievements, shared with colleagues, partners and the communities who use the product.

International

World Summit Awards (WSA)

A global programme, linked to the United Nations World Summit on the Information Society, that celebrates digital innovation with social impact. Kataho was recognised among the products honoured.

International

APICTA Awards

The Asia Pacific ICT Alliance Awards recognise outstanding ICT products across the region. Kataho was recognised on this regional stage.

National

ICT Social Innovation Awards

Recognition for technology that creates real social value. Kataho was honoured for its contribution.

Thank you

To the Addressgraph Nepal team and everyone who has supported Kataho.
